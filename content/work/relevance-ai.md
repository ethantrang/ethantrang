# Relevance AI

Title: Relevance AI
Slug: relevance-ai
Created At: 01/07/2024
Updated At: 16/04/2026

I worked as a Junior AI engineer at Relevance AI.

In July 2024, I was looking to get a proper job after not getting much success from startup project I was doing. I reached out to the founders on Linkedin and got myself an interview.

There, I worked on building AI agents for their business customers including TikTok, Airwallex, and SafetyCulture. Another thing I worked on (which was actually my "in" into the company) was building a Python developer SDK to use Relevance AI, learned a lot about building an agent building framework and developer experience.

I left to pursue working for myself in January 2025, knowing that I'd never reach my full potential had I remained comfortable there.
