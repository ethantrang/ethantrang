I set it up in April 2025, and since then (as of Nov 2025) we've done over 100k in revenue.

For the most part, we are a software development agency, specializing in 0-1 building of digital products, primarily web apps. Check out our work at [inflectlabs.co](https://inflectlabs.co/). We are looking to build our own products, you can sign up to our waitlist [here](https://inflectlabs.co/waitlist).

This will be my dream company one day. A small, talented, and obsessed team building things that make the world a little bit better. Many entrepreneurs out there have inspired me to take this path, and I hope my story will inpsire others one day.
